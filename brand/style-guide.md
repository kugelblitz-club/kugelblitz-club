# Kugelblitz Club / Brand guide

## Brand idea

Kugelblitz Club makes plasmoid healing feel tangible, human and alive. The visual language is cool, expressive and tactile: electric blue, deep indigo, soft violet and close-up imagery.

## Logo

`logo.svg` is the primary light-background wordmark. The current identity is intentionally typographic while the final symbol is being developed. Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the subtitle. Do not stretch, recolor, rotate or add effects to the logo.

Use `logo-light.svg` on the Indigo footer or other dark backgrounds.

## Color palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base | Indigo | `#182039` | Body text, dark sections, footer |
| Base | Mist | `#EEF0FA` | Main page background |
| Signal | Electric blue | `#5367D8` | Orbit mark, rules and links |
| Signal | Soft violet | `#7656D6` | Lightning mark, buttons and emphasis |
| Support | Periwinkle | `#66709A` | Secondary text |
| Support | Line | `#CDD3EB` | Rules, fields and dividers |

Use Indigo and Mist as the structural colors. Use Electric blue and Soft violet as focused signal colors.

## Typography

- **Manrope**: headings, navigation, buttons and body copy. Use bold weights with tight tracking for display text.
- **DM Mono**: labels, numbers, metadata and technical details.

## Icons

Use simple, single-purpose line or solid icons with rounded geometry. The Telegram icon in `icons/telegram.svg` uses the Acid light signal color and Ink glyph. Icons should sit beside a short action label rather than replace it.

## Imagery

The selected images in `imagery/` are close, dark and tactile. Favor images where hands, glass, light and plasma are visible. Use near-black backgrounds, crop confidently, and let the pink/violet light be the focal point. Avoid generic stock imagery, busy screenshots and unrelated lifestyle photography.

Selected:

- `plasma-hands.jpg`: strongest hero image; human interaction with the field.
- `plasma-orb.jpg`: clearest device/product image.
- `plasma-violet.jpg`: atmospheric supporting image.
